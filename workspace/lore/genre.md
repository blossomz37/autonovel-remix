# GENRE CONVENTIONS: COZY SUPERNATURAL HORROR

## Tone and Atmosphere
- **The Core Paradox:** The setting must feel genuinely comfortable (tea, armchairs, small-town routines) while the supernatural elements feel genuinely unsettling or dangerous.
- **The Horror:** The horror should be atmospheric, psychological, and uncanny, rather than gory or bleak.
- **The Coziness:** The coziness should come from relationship bonds, domestic rituals, and community, not from a lack of stakes.

## The World
- The supernatural is an accepted, if troublesome, part of reality.
- The community is small, insular, and has unspoken rules.
- There are ancient, perhaps decaying, institutions or agreements in place to manage the supernatural.

## The Mystery
- It must be solvable. Clues must be planted.
- The supernatural elements complicate the investigation but do not negate logic.
- The motive is deeply personal and rooted in the town's history or supernatural rules.

## The Protagonist
- Competent in their specific domain (e.g., running a B&B).
- Has a pragmatic, perhaps weary, relationship with the supernatural.
- Driven by a sense of responsibility to the community (both living and dead).
