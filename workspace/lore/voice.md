# THE NARRATIVE REGISTER

The prose should be atmospheric, slightly whimsical, but with an underlying edge of genuine supernatural dread.
Sentences should balance descriptions of mundane comforts (warm tea, crackling fires, soft wool) with unsettling intrusions (a cold draft that smells of sea salt and old blood, a shadow that lingers too long).
Do not lean into extreme gore or nihilism. 

# Part 1: GUARDRAILS (BANNED PHRASES)

Do not use these words:
- Suddenly
- Actually
- Very
- Just
- That
- Seemed to
- In a way that

# Part 2: THE POSITIVE DEFINITION

- **Tone**: Warm but paranoid. Like being inside a locked cabin while a storm rages outside.
- **Rhythm**: Pacing should be conversational and relaxed during the day, becoming tighter and more urgent when the sun sets.
- **Sensory**: Focus on contrasting the cozy (baking bread, crackling hearths) with the uncanny (the smell of ozone, the sudden drop in temperature, the sound of footsteps where no one walks).
