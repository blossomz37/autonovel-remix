## Magic System Rules

### Hard Rules (Tonal Law)

- Time is a fungible substance in Varanthe that can be borrowed. (seed.txt)
- The Ledger Houses lend years against future payment. (seed.txt)
- A borrower cannot borrow more than their natural remaining lifespan as collateral. (seed.txt)
- **DISCREPANCY:** seed.txt states the limit is natural remaining lifespan; world.md states "individuals may borrow years equivalent to their natural lifespan minus the years already lived" (e.g., a 30-year-old may borrow up to 50 years). These are consistent in effect but world.md specifies the formula more precisely.
- Assessors determine remaining lifespan by looking at the borrower — an intuitive, non-verbal perception. (seed.txt, world.md)
- Every contract must be signed in the presence of an Assessor. (world.md)
- Borrowed time accumulates experiential interest — the borrower owes specific experiences from their remaining life, chosen by the Ledger House. (seed.txt)
- The borrower does not know which experiences will be collected until the Assessor arrives and takes them. (seed.txt)
- After an experience is collected, the borrower remembers having lived it but the experience itself remembers nothing. (seed.txt) — *Note: phrasing in seed.txt is "You remember having lived them. They remember nothing" — meaning the experience is hollowed out.*
- Experiential interest is listed in the ledger in clinical language, e.g., *"One (1) instance: first experience of professional pride, borrower age 34. Collected."* (characters.md)
- A collected experience manifests for the borrower as a blurring — a memory they cannot quite locate. (characters.md)
- The borrower knows something has been taken after a collection. (characters.md)
- Borrowed time has a physical, perceptual effect on the borrower: colors appear one shade too saturated, sounds arrive half a beat late. (seed.txt)
- Longtime debtors develop a particular look: vivid and somehow adjacent to the moment they're inhabiting. (seed.txt)
- Borrowed time feels "slightly underwater." (seed.txt)
- Borrowed time feels "slippery and elusive." (world.md)
- The emotional weight of borrowed time can lead to psychological distress. (world.md)
- Upon the death of a debtor, the debt automatically transfers to their named heir. (seed.txt, world.md)
- Inherited debt transfer is law. (seed.txt)
- The heir must honor the contract signed by their predecessor. (world.md)
- The heir owes back years they never spent and experiences they never had, on a timeline the Ledger House sets. (seed.txt)
- Contracts with the Ledger Houses are unbreakable and must be honored regardless of circumstances. (world.md)
- Attempting to avoid repayment can lead to severe consequences including public shaming and further debt. (world.md)
- The magic is not dramatic or showy — it expresses as paperwork, clauses, and compound interest. (seed.txt)
- The horror of the system is described as bureaucratic; the violence as contractual. (seed.txt)
- The Ledger Houses have Assessors who can perceive remaining lifespan by looking at a person. (seed.txt)
- The assessment ability is described as intuitive and non-verbal. (world.md)
- Assessors often become emotionally detached as a professional consequence of viewing lives as numbers. (world.md)
- The ledgers themselves are physical books that smell like the specific borrower — their sweat, perfume, and something biological and old. (seed.txt)

### Soft Magic (Time Whispers)

- Soft magic manifests as "time whispers" — subtle echoes of past lives experienced by those burdened with debt. (world.md)
- Time whispers are felt as a haunting presence causing confusion and disorientation. (world.md)
- Each whisper carries the weight of an experience claimed by the Ledger House. (world.md)
- Those who hear whispers risk losing touch with reality as foreign memories blend with their own. (world.md)
- *Note: world.md labels this "Cass's Gift" in the section header — this appears to be a template error, as no character named Cass exists in this novel. The compiler's note in characters.md confirms "Cass Bellwright" belongs to a different story. The soft magic rules stand; the label "Cass's Gift" should not be used.*

## Cultural

- Temporal debt inheritance is law in Varanthe. (seed.txt)
- **Debt Anniversaries:** Families commemorate the day they borrowed time with a feast and sharing stories of lives changed by the decision to borrow. The day is both celebration and mourning. (world.md)
- **Taboo:** It is considered deeply disrespectful to speak ill of the deceased, especially in cases where debts remain unpaid. Such talk is believed to invite bad luck and more debt. (world.md)
- **The Tide of Time:** An annual festival celebrating the river Seru. (world.md)
- At the Tide of Time, families gather to honor those lost to debt and lives lived on borrowed time. (world.md)
- At the Tide of Time, lanterns are set afloat on the river, symbolizing hopes for the future. (world.md)
- The Assessor's sash is a professional garment worn by Assessors. (characters.md)
- Cedar oil is used to preserve the oldest ledgers. (characters.md)
- The neighborhood healer is an established role in Varanthe society. (characters.md)
- The Ledger Houses use a specific official notation system recorded in black ink. (characters.md)
- Borrowers may annotate their own ledgers in the margins. (characters.md)
